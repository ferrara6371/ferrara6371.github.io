About Me

Hi, I’m Joseph Ferrara. I’ve been drawn to entertainment and media since Thanksgiving evening in 2019 after working on a short film with my brother and best friend. Since then, I’ve dedicated my time to studying Film & TV at Montclair State University and expect to graduate in Spring of ‘27.

My interests lie primarily in writing and directing but have not limited me to pursue electrical work vocationally both on film sets and as of recent, as a lighting technician and designer for live theatre. My interest in the performing arts has really culminated as I’ve dedicated the better part of the past year to developing my skills in headshot photography, having aided over one hundred performers to legitimize their image for auditions and online presence.

I’ve also established an online hub for upcoming talent in film alongside some high school friends through Small Room Pictures, which I’ve attached to my filmography thus far and have grown a digital presence reaching thousands. It’s my intention as a storyteller and admirer of digital and live performing arts to curate purposeful images both moving and still, that connect people and reestablish community amongst up-and-coming artists.
